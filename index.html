▍China Watch · China

July Politburo meeting sets the Fifth Plenum for October and signals incremental support

The Politburo met on 30 July and scheduled the Fifth Plenum of the 20th Central Committee for October in Beijing, with the main agenda "major issues concerning the unremitting advancement of full and rigorous Party self-governance" — notable as the last plenum before next year's 21st Party Congress.

Sinocism · 30 July

Readout language hints at rising economic anxiety

The economic section says it is "necessary to attach great importance to the difficulties and challenges in economic performance", and calls to "promptly plan and introduce practical and effective incremental policies" — Bill Bishop reads this as a signal of at least marginal additional support. The risk section leads on stabilising the property market, the debt-resolution package, reform of local small and medium financial institutions, and capital-market resilience; the trade section pushes services trade and "balanced trade development".
