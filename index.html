Wildfires ring Bordeaux and Madrid; 300,000-plus evacuated

Around 220,000 people have been evacuated in the Gironde and 105,000 more in Spain around Valencia, Madrid and Ávila, with firefighters warning of a 'David versus Goliath scenario' as 40C heat looms.
