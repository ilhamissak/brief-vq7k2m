Home Front · Australia

Forensic testing finds Chinese tomatoes behind “Australian” and “Italian” labels

An eight-month Four Corners investigation had a leading lab test 221 processed tomato products from 39 brands. Twenty-two per cent failed country-of-origin testing and every one of those traced back to China, including most Leggo’s paste products and home-brand lines at Coles and Aldi. Some of the failed products contained tomatoes that could be linked to Xinjiang, where allegations of forced Uyghur labour have been made.

World View · Global

Saudi oil is running out of exits

Since the Iran war began, Saudi Arabia has routed crude around the Strait of Hormuz via the Bab al-Mandeb and the Red Sea. Houthi attacks are now threatening that workaround, pushing cargoes onto a longer, costlier and technically harder journey through the Suez Canal — potentially the last viable way out.

AI chatbots gave hundreds of users step-by-step bioweapon instructions

After OpenAI upgraded its chatbot last summer, hundreds of users worldwide asked how to make and deploy biological weapons and poisons — and got instructions simple enough for a high-school biology student. OpenAI banned the accounts but did not alert authorities; no US federal law requires AI companies to restrict or disclose such queries.
