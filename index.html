'21st-century slaves' are picking Australia's fruit and vegetables

Four Corners' continuing investigation into the food industry has spoken to Pacific Island workers offered jobs with no sick leave, no pay slips and no super — including one blueberry farm where workers wore nappies because the employer would not allow bathroom breaks. Gershom Isaac came from Papua New Guinea chasing a better life and instead joined tens of thousands of migrant workers trapped in a system that keeps Australian food cheap. The full investigation, Duped: Food Exposed, is on ABC iview.
