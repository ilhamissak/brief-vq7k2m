Home Front · Australia

AstraZeneca backs down, will supply Zoladex free indefinitely

After a months-long standoff with the federal government over the PBS price it called "unsustainable", AstraZeneca will now give Australian women the breast cancer and endometriosis drug at no cost, indefinitely. Patients had been left in limbo for months — some contemplating having their ovaries removed — and say they still fear the company could reverse course again.
